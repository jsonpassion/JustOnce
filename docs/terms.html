1. 서비스 소개

딱 한 번(Just Once, 이하 "앱")은 TEPS를 준비하는 한국어 사용자가 예문을 한 번만 듣고 핵심 표현의 뜻을 고르며 어휘를 익히도록 돕는, 독립적으로 개발된 iOS 앱입니다. 앱은 TEPS를 주관하는 서울대학교 TEPS관리위원회와 관계가 없습니다. 앱은 점수대 네 단계의 예문 카드 3,943장, 문항마다 도는 초시계, 놓친 표현 다시 듣기를 제공합니다. 처음 7일(공부한 날 기준)은 제한 없이 무료이고, 그 뒤에는 하루 이용량 안에서 무료이며, 구독(이용권)으로 하루 제한을 없앨 수 있습니다.

  • 예문과 표현을 읽는 음성은 사람이 녹음한 것이 아니라 AI 음성 모델로 미리 만든 것입니다. 음성이 없는 문장은 기기에 내장된 음성으로 읽습니다.
  • 카드는 시험의 듣기와 어휘 문항을 참고해 개발자가 만든 연습용 문장이며, 실제 기출문제가 아닙니다. 오답 보기는 같은 권의 다른 표현 뜻입니다.
  • 초시계의 25초는 TEPS 어휘와 문법 영역의 문항당 평균 시간을 참고한 값이며, 공식 기준이 아닙니다. 점수대는 참고용 구분이며 공식 점수나 판정이 아닙니다.

English

1. About the service

딱 한 번 (Just Once, "the App") is an independently developed iOS application that helps Korean speakers preparing for TEPS learn vocabulary by hearing an example sentence once and choosing the meaning of its key expression. The App is not affiliated with the TEPS Council of Seoul National University, which administers TEPS. The App provides 3,943 sentence cards in four score bands, a stopwatch on every question, and a replay of missed expressions. The first seven study days are free without limit; after that the App stays free within a daily allowance, and a subscription (a pass) removes the daily limit.

2. Free use and in-app purchases

  • Free: the first seven days on which you study are free without limit. After that, 20 cards a day stay free, with every band and feature still available. Clearing the study record does not restart the free week, and on the same iCloud account it carries over a reinstall.
  • Paid: a pass is a monthly subscription or a yearly subscription; either removes the daily limit on every card. The scope of a pass you have already bought is never reduced.
  • Auto-renewing subscriptions (monthly, yearly): unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, a subscription renews for the same period at the same price, and your Apple account is charged within 24 hours before the period ends. You can cancel at any time in iOS Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions, and you keep access until the paid period ends. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.
  • Price changes: subscription price changes are announced in advance under Apple's policies, and an increase is not applied to existing subscribers without their consent.
  • Prices are those shown in the App Store and may vary by country, exchange rate and promotion.
  • Payment is handled through your Apple account; no sign-up or login is required.
  • Purchases can be restored with "Restore purchases" on another device signed in to the same Apple account, or after reinstalling the App.
  • Refunds, including subscription refunds, follow the Apple App Store's refund policy. The Developer cannot process refunds directly; request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

3. Study data

Your band, rounds, per-card records and answer times are stored on your device and in your own iCloud key-value storage. The Developer collects or stores none of your study data on any server. "Clear study record" in the App clears the whole record and cannot be undone.

4. Content and voice

  • Every sentence, meaning and voice clip is inside the App; studying needs no internet connection. Content may be added or corrected in App updates.
  • The voice that reads sentences and expressions was not recorded by a person; it was made in advance with an AI voice model. Sentences without a clip are read by the device's built-in voice.
  • The cards are practice sentences written by the Developer with the test's listening and vocabulary items in mind; they are not actual test questions. Wrong options are meanings of other expressions of the same book.
  • The stopwatch's 25 seconds follows the average time per question in the TEPS vocabulary and grammar section for reference; it is not an official standard. Score bands are a reference, not an official score or judgment.
  • TEPS is administered by the TEPS Council of Seoul National University. "TEPS" is used in the App and its store listing only to say which test the content prepares for; the App is not affiliated with, sponsored, endorsed or approved by it.

5. Disclaimer

The App is a study aid and does not guarantee any score. Round records and answer speed are practice aids.
